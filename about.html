About Bett Consultancy

Helping Kenyan business owners look as good online as they do in person.

Our Story

Bett Consultancy started with a simple observation: too many good businesses in Kenya were being held back by outdated websites or no online presence at all. We set out to fix that — one business at a time, combining solid web development with design that actually reflects the brand behind it.

Mission, Vision & Values

Mission

To give every business owner we work with a website and brand that earns trust the moment someone sees it.

Vision

A Kenya where every local business, regardless of size, has a professional presence online.

Values

Honesty in pricing, reliability in delivery, and craftsmanship in every line of code and every design.
